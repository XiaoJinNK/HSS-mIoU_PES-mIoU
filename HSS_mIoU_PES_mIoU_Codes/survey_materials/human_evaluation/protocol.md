# Protocol

## Participants

- Recommended sample size: 20 to 30 participants.
- Run the semantic block first, then the structural block.
- Add a short break between blocks.

## Quality Checks

- Practice items can flag participants who misunderstand the task.
- Hidden repeat items are used to measure response consistency.
- Low-consistency participants can be reported separately or excluded with a predeclared rule.

## Primary Analysis

- Semantic task: agreement between participant preferences and the semantic metric preference on core items.
- Structural task: agreement between participant preferences and the structural metric preference on core items.
- Secondary summaries: tie-as-half agreement, confidence-weighted agreement, repeat consistency, and practice accuracy.
